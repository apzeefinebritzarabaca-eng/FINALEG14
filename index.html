07 / OUR ADVOCACY

TOO MANY FISH!

🐟🐟🐟🐟🐟🐟🐟

WHAT IS OVERCROWDING?

Fish overcrowding happens when too many fish are kept in a tank that does not provide enough space and resources for their needs.

Some fish owners add more fish to save space or accommodate more animals. However, a tank has limits, and ignoring them can harm aquatic life.

THE REALITY

A tank is not healthy just because it contains water and fish. Its size, water quality, filtration, and the needs of each species matter.

SPACE MATTERS!
08 / THE CONSEQUENCES

THE HIDDEN PROBLEM!

💧

POOR WATER QUALITY

More fish produce more waste, which can cause harmful ammonia buildup.

😟

STRESS

Limited space and competition can make fish stressed.

🦠

DISEASE RISKS

Poor conditions and stress can increase the risk of illness.

🌊

LESS SPACE

Fish may not have enough room to swim and behave naturally.

REMEMBER!

More fish does not automatically mean a better aquarium.

09 / RESPONSIBLE FISH CARE

GIVE THEM ROOM!

🐠 💙 🐟

HOW CAN WE HELP?

  • Research each fish species before buying.
  • Choose an appropriate tank size.
  • Check water quality regularly.
  • Maintain proper filtration.
  • Avoid keeping more fish than the aquarium can support.
